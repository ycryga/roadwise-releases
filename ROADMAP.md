# Roadwise roadmap

What's being worked on, what's next, and ideas for later. Plans change with what drivers ask for:
send yours with **💡 Request a feature** in the app or in the [browser version](https://ycryga.github.io/roadwise-releases/).
What's already out is in the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md).

## 🚧 Working on now

- **Google Play Store**: so Roadwise installs and updates like any other app, without allowing
  unknown sources. Starts with a closed test.
- **Android Auto**: turn-by-turn directions, arrival time and voice on your car's screen, plus Home,
  Work and recent places. Needs the Play Store version to show in cars for everyone.

## ⏭️ Next

- **Roadwise's own routing server**, so routes don't depend on free public servers when many
  people drive with Roadwise at once.
- **More in the browser version**: Home and Work, speed cameras, tolls and speed limits.
- **More accurate arrival times**, using how long drives really take.

## 💭 Ideas for later

- **Faster roads learned from drivers**: which back roads beat the highway at rush hour, without
  recording anyone's trips.
- **An iPhone app** (the browser version works on iPhone today).

## Support

Roadwise is free, with no ads. Donations (DuitNow or [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/ycryga)) pay for live
traffic and the servers: **☕ Support Roadwise** in the app shows this month's goal.
