Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL):
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

The on-phone routing map is built from OpenStreetMap data and is itself available under the ODbL;
how it is built is described in the Mana source (routing/config.yml).

Live traffic, the traffic map, accidents, roadworks, closures, extra speed limits and places:
© TomTom (https://www.tomtom.com).

Map style and tiles: OpenFreeMap (https://openfreemap.org), using OpenMapTiles
(https://openmaptiles.org). Map tiles © OpenMapTiles, data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Routes, when no routing map on the phone covers the trip (and always in the browser version): OSRM,
the Open Source Routing Machine (https://project-osrm.org), on its demo server.

Speed limits, tolls and lane arrows along a route: Valhalla (https://github.com/valhalla/valhalla)
on FOSSGIS's server (https://fossgis.de), and the Overpass API. Place search: Photon by komoot
(https://photon.komoot.io), with Nominatim as a backup. All on OpenStreetMap data.


PRIVACY

Mana has no accounts and does not collect your name, phone number or contacts.
To work, it sends the following to these services:

- Place searches, with your rough location to rank results: Photon by komoot (photon.komoot.io), or
  OpenStreetMap's Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org) when Photon is down. A spot you long-press
  or pick on the map goes to Photon too, to name it after the nearest street.
- Your route's start, stops and destination (and possible stops, to say how much time each adds),
  when no routing map on the phone covers the trip: the OSRM demo server (router.project-osrm.org).
- A short map link you share to Mana (maps.app.goo.gl): Google, to find where it points.
- The path of your chosen route, for speed limits, tolls and lane arrows: Valhalla by FOSSGIS
  (valhalla1.openstreetmap.de).
- The area around your route, for speed cameras, toll plazas and speed bumps: the Overpass API.
- Map tiles for the area you are looking at: OpenFreeMap.
- The path of each route on offer, the area you're looking at or driving through, and searches you
  press search for (with your rough location): Mana's server (on Supabase), which asks TomTom for
  live traffic, incidents, speed limits, the traffic map and more places. It doesn't keep them
  or anything about you, and TomTom sees Mana's server, not your phone.
- Reports you choose to send (type and position), your "still there?" answers, and toll amounts you
  report, with a random ID made by the app: Mana's database on Supabase. Reports stop showing
  on the map after a few hours but are kept in the database. The area of your trip, to read other
  drivers' usual waits there (nothing about you is sent).
- Feature requests you send: only the text you type, on Mana's database on Supabase.
- Places you add for everyone: the name, kind and position, on Mana's database on Supabase, and "not here" answers. If you tick it, the same goes to OpenStreetMap as an anonymous public note.
- Only if you say yes to "Share stop times" (asked once, on your first drive): where you waited while navigating (position rounded to
  about 11 m, direction, time of week in eight bands, how long), with no ID, trip or date, and
  nothing from the first and last 300 m of a drive: Mana's database on Supabase. Also, when a
  drive's arrival time was more than 5 minutes off: the time shown, the time it took, its length in
  km and the time of week (no positions), to check how accurate arrival times are.

Your saved places, recent searches and the stops your phone notices stay on your phone.

While navigating, Mana keeps using your location with the screen off or another app in front
(shown by the "Navigating" notification) so directions keep coming; it stops when you press Exit.
